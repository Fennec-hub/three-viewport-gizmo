The `rounded-cube` is one seamless closed rounded box. Its 26 targets are pieces of that box with no gap or seam between them, each cut along the same rule the hit test classifies a pointer by, so hovering lights exactly the piece the pointer is over. The box draws at most 15,000 triangles at any `smoothness`. Its corners now default to the edges' colour (`0xeeeeee`), so its edges and corners share one idle material.
